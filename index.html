What's New in v2.0 🎵

A New Era for Offline Music Players. Melodika introduces one of the most advanced offline lyric systems ever built.

✨ Major Features

  • Intelligent Lyrics Hub: A completely redesigned screen for seamless lyric and queue navigation.
  • Phonetic Pronunciation Support: Sing along effortlessly with Hinglish/Romanized guides.
  • Smart Artwork Engine: Automatically fetches and embeds missing high-definition album art directly in Settings.
  • On-Demand Lyrics Fetcher: Automatically retrieves and embeds missing lyrics into your tracks for permanent offline access.
  • The Lyric Lab Engine: Our proprietary engine now flawlessly supports Simple, Line-by-Line, and ultra-precise Word-by-Word synchronization.
  • Karaoke Mode: Immerse yourself with word-synced lyrics that glow flawlessly in real-time.
  • Refined Playback Controls: A smarter, decluttered layout designed for effortless navigation.
  • Aesthetic Color Palette: Handpicked hues for a calming, luxurious visual experience.
  • Adaptive Lyric Layouts: Custom-tailored premium views for all three lyric formats.
  • Custom Playlist Iconography: Distinct visual identities for your curated collections.
  • Redesigned Splash Experience: A beautiful, welcoming launch screen.
  • Extended Tag Editor: Now fully supports metadata editing for M4A audio formats!

🚀 Improvements & Optimizations

  • Visual Consistency: Resolved artwork scaling issues across Queue and Lyric views.
  • Lightning Fast Launch: Significantly reduced startup times.
  • Seamless Onboarding: Eliminated blank screens during initial launch and first-time installations.
  • Precision Playback: Corrected seek bar and timing overlaps for flawless scrubbing.
  • Optimized Battery Usage: Enjoy your music longer with under-the-hood power efficiency improvements.
  • Enhanced Stability: Addressed unexpected closures for uninterrupted, stable listening.
